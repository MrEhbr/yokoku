# Upcoming

**Upcoming** is a calendar of what comes out for monitored items: episode air
dates and movie release dates, grouped by day.

## Periods

**Coming up**
:   From today through the next year. The default.

**Week**
:   Monday through Sunday.

**Month**
:   The calendar month.

Week and Month have arrows for the previous and next period, and **Today**
to come back to the current one. They include the days of the period that
have passed, so a week view also shows what came out on Monday.

## Entries

Each day has a heading with how far away it is: *today*, *tomorrow*, *in 3
days*. An entry shows:

- the item, linked to its page;
- the episode, like *S01E05 Title*, or the kind of movie release: *Cinema
  release*, *Digital release* or *Physical release*;
- its file status: **Downloaded**, **Missing** or **Upcoming**.

A movie shows once for each of its release dates.

Only monitored items appear, and an episode only when its season and series
are monitored too. Dates follow the **Time zone** setting.
