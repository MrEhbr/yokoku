# Wanted

**Wanted** lists what you're missing: monitored episodes that have aired and
monitored movies that are out, without a file. The number on its menu entry
counts them.

## What Counts as Missing

An **episode** is missing when:

- it is monitored, and so are its season and series,
- it has no file,
- and it aired before today. An episode counts as aired from the day after
  its air date; one without an air date never shows.

A **movie** is missing when it is monitored, has no file, and its digital or
physical release date has come. A cinema release alone doesn't count: there
is nothing to download yet.

"Today" follows the **Time zone** setting, or the server's own when it's
empty.

## Reading the Page

The top line sums it up, like *12 episodes in 3 series and 2 movies*.

Each series is a group, closed at first, with the number of missing episodes
and the latest one. Open it for a table of the missing episodes, newest first,
with their air dates. **Movies** follow at the end.

To get something off the list, download it from its page with
[**Add torrent**](./items.md#add-a-torrent), or stop monitoring it there if
you don't want it.
